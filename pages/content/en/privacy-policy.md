### What information is recorded?
When you email us, we receive your email address, message, and information you choose to provide. The website also remembers your light or dark display choice and language on your device; you can remove these in your browser settings.

### How is your information used?
Information you send by email is used only to read and answer your request. We do not use it for advertising or unwanted messages.

### How does md2site use GitHub and Cloudflare?
GitHub is where Bee Studio SnS stores the source code and publishes website updates. [See how GitHub protects information](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Cloudflare receives updates from GitHub and sends the website to visitors. When you open a page, Cloudflare may record your network address, access time, and the page opened to keep the service running and block harmful access. [See how Cloudflare protects information](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

### Is your information shared?
Bee Studio SnS does not sell or rent your information. Email you send is handled only by Bee Studio SnS and the email service. GitHub and Cloudflare record only the information needed to operate the website, as explained above. Information is provided to authorities only when legally required and is kept only as long as needed.

### What are your choices?
You may ask to view, correct, or delete information you provided unless the law requires retention. Email `support@beestudio.icu`.
